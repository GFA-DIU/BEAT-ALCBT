# Code of conduct

BEAT is used and built by people in many countries, organisations and
professions. We want taking part to be a good experience for everyone,
whatever their background, experience, language or identity.

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

**Do:** be respectful and patient, assume good intent, give feedback on the
work rather than the person, and accept correction gracefully, especially on
numbers and sources.

**Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone; make sexual or discriminatory
comments; publish someone's private information; or keep contacting someone
who has asked you to stop.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions)
and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to <kontakt@heat-international.de>. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or ban
anyone who breaks this code, as described in the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
